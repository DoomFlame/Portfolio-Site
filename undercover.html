Undercover

This was a game based on picking the right clothing for spies so that they blend in to their mission environment. Our goal was to help those with learning disabilities determine what is appropriate attire for different scenarios. I did most of the programming for this game, including the UI interaction, scene changes, sound manager, game manager and NPC dialogue.

It was a great experience being able to interact and ask questions to the Simcoach Games development team, and get an insight into the mindset they have towards making games. Designing games with a specific purpose aside from the sole reason of enjoyment is very rewarding, as it feels like the games make a significant positive impact on those that play them.
