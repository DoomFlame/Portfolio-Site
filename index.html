HIGHLIGHTED PROJECT

Just Bite Back (2025)

Just Bite Back was a project that I worked as part of my high school senior year game production course during the second semester, although my team had been slightly planning our game during the first semester of the school year. The premise of the game was a PvP FPS game in which you first cook various foods in your customizable food truck, then use that food to fight other players, with different effects depending on the kind of food and the quality it was cooked at.

Due to the small team size (3 people) and the small amount of time we had in class, we were only able to prototype the outline of the FPS part of the game. I was the sole programmer for this prototype, in which I was able to implement a multiplayer system using Unity Lobby and Relay. If I had the opportunity to work on any project with no limitations and a full team, I would definitely want to flesh out this game more.

Undercover

This was a game based on picking the right clothing for spies so that they blend in to their mission environment. Our goal was to help those with learning disabilities determine what is appropriate attire for different scenarios.

I did most of the programming for this game, including the UI interaction, scene changes, and NPC dialogue.
