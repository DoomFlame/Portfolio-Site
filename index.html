ABOUT ME

Hello! I'm Zach, an aspiring game developer from Pittsburgh. I study Computer Science with a minor in Game Design at Carnegie Mellon University. I have been in the game design world since my Freshman year of highschool, where I took multiple courses relating to game design, such as animation, music development, and multiple game development courses, leading to me graduating with an Associate's Degree in Multimedia Programming, Simulation, and Gaming from the Community College of Allegheny County.

Here, you will see a collection of the projects I have worked on and a summary of what I did for them. Click on each project to get a more in depth look at the project, accompanied at download links if possible. My goal is to someday contribute to a game that changes the people who play it, whether that's something deep or just a way to make them smile!
